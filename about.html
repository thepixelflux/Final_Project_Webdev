Who We Are

Founded in 2022, The Data Alchemists is the official data science club of Manipal Institute of Technology (MIT), based in Manipal, Karnataka. Comprised of enthusiastic students and emerging professionals, we’ve cultivated a dynamic platform where passionate learners come together to collaborate, explore, and innovate in the world of data.


What We Do

  • Education & Upskilling
    Host workshops, study groups, and mentorship programs in key fields:
    • Data Science, Machine Learning, Deep Learning (NLP & Vision)
    • Data Engineering, Big Data, Data Visualization
    • Web & App Development, Data Structures & Algorithms
  • Bootcamps & Events
    Organize annual flagship events like:
    • Summer Bootcamp 2025 — Free certification in Data Analytics, ML, Deep Learning, Web/App Development, and DSA for first-year students.
    • DataVista 2024 — A 2-week expedition with workshops, live Q&As, and interactive sessions with data science experts.
  • Projects & Skill Application
    • Support real-world work through group projects and internships (e.g., a Data Engineer co-op with Amazon Robotics working on robotic-arm data at scale).
    • Encourage collaboration on portfolio-building, Kaggle-style challenges, and hackathons.
  • Community Engagement
    Maintain an open community that embraces students from all backgrounds through:
    • Roadmaps
    • Cohort-based learning
    • Peer support and mentoring

Our Outlook

TDA’s vision extends beyond campus—its goal is to empower the MIT community with data-driven capabilities that prepare members for the evolving tech landscape. Through accessible learning, hands-on projects, and a supportive environment, they aim to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry demands, and cultivate tomorrow’s data leaders.
